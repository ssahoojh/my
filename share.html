Toy Sharing Guidelines for Jay

For non-electric toys, you may share with anyone who asks. Since they do not use batteries, you can enjoy them again after others are finished playing.

Electric Ride-On Sharing Rules

You are encouraged to share your electric toys with kids who:

  • Are kind, respectful, and friendly.
  • Invite you to play, especially when you are by yourself.
  • Talk with you, listen to you, and respond when you speak.
  • Include you in games and activities.
  • Are new to the neighborhood and would like a chance to make friends.

Think Carefully Before Sharing If:

  • Someone only talks to you when they want to use your electric toys.
  • Someone ignores you when you try to talk or ask a question.
  • Someone says they will play with you only after getting a turn on your toy.
  • You feel that the toy is the only reason the person is spending time with you.
  • If someone consistently said "no" when you asked to play with their toys.

Most Important Rule

Share because you want to be kind, not because you feel pressured to. Good friends enjoy spending time with you even when no toys are involved.

Remember

Real friends are not perfect.

  • Sometimes they may disagree with you.
  • Sometimes they may get upset or angry.
  • Sometimes you may argue or have a small fight.
  • Sometimes they may not want to play the same game as you.

That is normal. What matters is that they still respect you, include you, and enjoy spending time with you.

  • Good friends do not regularly exclude you.
  • Good friends do not make you feel invisible.
  • Good friends do not pretend you do not exist.
  • Good friends like you for who you are, not just for your toys.

A friend does not have to agree with you all the time. A good friend is someone who still wants to be around you even when you disagree, make mistakes, or have a bad day.
